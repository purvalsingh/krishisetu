# PPT 7 — illustration brief

**Why only pictures:** the SIH template's own instructions slide says to keep the deck
to six slides, to avoid paragraphs, and to present the idea in points, diagrams,
infographics and pictures, using the provided template. So the images supply the
pictures and nothing else — the template supplies the title, logo, footer, slide number
and team oval, and the numbers go on in PowerPoint afterwards.

Do not draw a slide title, the SIH logo, the blue footer band, a slide number or the
team oval into any image. A drawn copy collides with the real one and breaks the format.


Generate **separate spot illustrations**, one object per image. Nothing else.

## Absolute rules

- **No text of any kind.** No words, no letters, no numbers, no rupee symbols, no
  labels, no captions, no watermarks. Every figure and every word goes on in
  PowerPoint afterwards, where it stays sharp and correct.
- **One subject per image**, centred, isolated on a plain white background.
- Flat vector illustration. Soft pastel fills, thin darker outlines, rounded shapes.
  No photorealism, no gradients, no drop shadows, no 3D, no background scenery.
- Square, 1024 × 1024.
- **Same palette and same line weight across all of them** so they read as one set:
  sage green, slate blue, warm amber, soft plum, light grey, on white.
- Indian context where people appear: kurta, turban, saree, tempo truck, kirana shop.

Name the files as given below.
